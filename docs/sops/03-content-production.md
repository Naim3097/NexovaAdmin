# SOP 3 — Content Production

**Owner:** Bob (video) / Pokjak (design) · **Board:** nexops.my/content

Every content card carries four status lights: **Direction · Headline · Draft ·
Approved**. The lights only tell the truth if these steps happen on the card.

## Steps

1. **Direction** (AM or client) — every card gets a written direction before any
   production. Cards the agency creates proactively: the AM writes the direction
   on the card (light 1 turns on). No direction, no production.
2. **Assignee** — whoever picks up the card sets themselves as **assignee**
   immediately. Unassigned work is invisible work.
3. **Headline / visual idea** (Pokjak or Bob) — fill Visual Headline before
   producing (light 2). For carousels set the **visual count** — each visual is
   one quota unit and drives billing.
4. **Draft** (Bob/Pokjak) — upload via the card's uploader (handles video sizes).
   Submitting turns light 3 on and notifies the client portal.
5. **Revisions** — client change requests turn light 3 yellow. Stay within the
   client's revision limit; the system counts. At the limit, the AM tells the
   client further rounds are billable (price is on the client record).
6. **Approved** (client, in portal) — light 4. Schedule the post date/time on the
   card; mark posted when live.

## Rules

- Never send drafts over WhatsApp for review — approval must happen in the portal
  or it doesn't count against revisions/quota.
- Quota overage is flagged automatically on the card and in the AM's weekly
  update — the AM raises billable extras with the client, not production.
